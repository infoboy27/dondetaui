# Paid promo: "Te avisamos de las ofertas reales"

Goal: account sign-ups before Black Friday (digest email, PR #69).

- **Creatives:** `bf-ad-registro-feed.jpg` (4:5) and `bf-ad-registro-story.jpg` (9:16), generated by `make_signup_ad.py`.
- **Video (recommended for the paid promo):** `bf-ad-reel.mp4` (9:16, ~23 s, Dominican voiceover "Mario" from ElevenLabs, burned-in subtitles) and `bf-ad-reel-cover.jpg`, generated by `make_reel_ad.py` (uses the live price check; voice clips cached in `source/voice/`). Add music from Meta's sound library when creating the ad. Prices in it are from 2026-11-13: re-run before launch if they changed (the script stops if the example product changed).
- **Caption:** `caption-ad-registro.txt`.
- **Button / destination URL (Meta Ads, "Registrarte"):**
  `https://dondeta.com.do/cuenta/black-friday?activar=1&utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=black-friday-2026`
  (or generate it in /admin → Instagram → Campañas so it shows up in the report).
- **Before turning it on:** Resend live (otherwise nothing is sent), migration 031 in prod, and PR #69 merged. Test the link in an incognito window: sign up → confirmation screen.
- **Suggested budget and dates:** Nov 17–26, DR, ages 22–55, interests: shopping/offers/home appliances; start at ~US$5/day, check cost per sign-up after 48 h and raise or cut. Cut it off on Nov 27 (from then on, the "today" posts do the work).
- **Measure:** sign-ups in the owner metrics (Perfil) and traffic by `utm_campaign=black-friday-2026` in /admin → Campañas.
- **Bio link (organic):** during the campaign, point the Instagram bio to the same URL with `utm_medium=bio`.
